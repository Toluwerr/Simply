Q: What sound does a duck make?
A: A duck says 'quack'.

Q: What sound does a snake make?
A: A snake says 'hiss'.

Q: What is the opposite of 'early'?
A: 'late' is the opposite of 'light'.

Q: What is the Roman numeral for 22?
A: The Roman numeral for 22 is xxii.

Q: How many days are in February?
A: February has 28 days, and 29 in a leap year.
